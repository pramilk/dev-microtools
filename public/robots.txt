# robots.txt for https://devmicrotools.com

# Policy, in one line: everything on this site is public and crawlable, AI assistants
# that identify themselves and follow the rules are welcome, and crawlers with a known
# record of ignoring robots.txt, hammering origins, or reselling scraped content are not.
#
# Note on precedence: a crawler obeys the single most specific group that names it and
# ignores every other group, including `*`. So the explicit `Allow: /` groups below are
# not redundant with the default — without them, a bot named in this file would fall
# through to `*`, and more importantly the list documents an intentional decision rather
# than leaving "are AI crawlers allowed here?" to be inferred from silence.
#
# An LLM-oriented site summary lives at https://devmicrotools.com/llms.txt
