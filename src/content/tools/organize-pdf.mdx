  - Click the magnifier on a thumbnail to check a page at full size. Use the arrow keys to step through the pages.

faq:
  - q: Is my PDF uploaded to a server?
    a: No. The PDF is read and rewritten entirely in your browser, in a background worker so the page stays responsive, and the thumbnails are drawn on your device too. Nothing is transmitted anywhere. You can disconnect from the internet after the page loads and the tool still works.
  - q: How do I move several pages at once?
    a: Select them first. Click one page, then Shift-click another to select everything in between, or Ctrl-click style toggling by clicking pages one at a time. Then press Earlier or Later to move the whole selection one place at a time. Pages that were spread out gather together as they move. To move a single page a long way, it is quicker to drag it and drop it on the left or right half of the page it should go before or after.
  - q: What does Collate duplex scan do?
    a: It fixes the page order you get when you scan double-sided paper on a scanner that only scans one side. You scan all the fronts, turn the stack over and scan all the backs, which come out from the last sheet to the first. Collate duplex scan interleaves the two halves into front 1, back 1, front 2, back 2 and so on. If the result looks wrong, press Undo.
  - q: How does Find blank pages decide a page is blank?
    a: It draws each page small and measures how much of it is ink, ignoring a thin border where scanners often pick up dark edges. A page with almost no ink, such as an empty back or a separator sheet with a few specks of dust, counts as blank. The pages it finds are selected rather than deleted, so you can check them with the magnifier and press Remove when you are happy. A page with only a page number or a single short line is not treated as blank.
  - q: Can I add page numbers, a watermark or a background?
    a: Yes. Page numbers can go at any corner or the middle of the top or bottom edge, in formats like "3", "Page 3" or "Page 3 of 12", starting from any number and from any page, so a cover can stay unnumbered. Header or footer text and a watermark are drawn the same way, upright even on rotated pages. A background colour or JPG or PNG image is drawn underneath each page's content. Scanned pages, and pages that paint their own white background, cover it. The text uses the PDF built-in Helvetica font, which covers Western European letters, digits and common symbols.
  - q: Will bookmarks, links and form fields still work?
    a: Yes. The pages are copied as a group with the same engine as Merge PDF and Split PDF, so bookmarks are re-pointed at each page's new position, links between pages you kept still land on the right page, and form fields stay fillable with their current values. A link or bookmark pointing at a page you removed cannot work, so it is removed rather than left pointing nowhere. If an inserted PDF has a form field with the same name as one in your document, it is renamed so the two do not share a value.
  - q: Can I organize password-protected or digitally signed PDFs?
    a: Encrypted PDFs, meaning those with an open password or permission restrictions, cannot be rewritten in a browser. Open such a file in a PDF viewer, use "Print to PDF" to save an unprotected copy, and organize that instead. Signed PDFs can be organized, but a digital signature only covers the exact original file, so it will not show as valid in the new one. The tool warns you when that happens.

## Page edits and page stamps

The tool works in two layers. The **page grid** is where the document's structure changes:
order, rotation, which pages are in it, and where blank pages or pages from other PDFs go.
Every edit there goes onto an undo history, so trying something costs nothing. The
**Add to the pages** options are then drawn onto the finished pages: page numbers, a header
or footer line, a watermark, and a background. They count pages in the new order, and they
are positioned against each page as it is displayed, so a page you rotated still gets its
number at the bottom rather than up the side.

## Why a rewrite, not a re-print

Many online page organizers rebuild the PDF by copying each page in isolation, which breaks
internal links and drops bookmarks, or by turning pages into images, which makes text
unselectable and files bigger. This tool rewrites the document with the same page-assembly
engine as Merge PDF and Split PDF. Pages keep their text, fonts and images exactly as they
were, so the result is usually about the same size as the original. Compression is optional
and uses the PDF Compressor's presets, applied to the finished file.

## What powers this tool

PDF parsing and writing use [`pdf-lib`](https://github.com/Hopding/pdf-lib) (MIT licensed),
in a background Web Worker that is only loaded once you choose a file. Thumbnails, the
full-size page view and blank-page detection use [`pdf.js`](https://github.com/mozilla/pdf.js)
(Apache 2.0 licensed), which is downloaded when you open your first PDF and is given the file
directly, so it never leaves your device either. The preview of the result is your browser's
own PDF viewer.
