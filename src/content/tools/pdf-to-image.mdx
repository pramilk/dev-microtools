  - q: Should I choose JPEG or PNG?
    a: JPEG for most things. It gives much smaller files, which is what you want for email, chat and the web, and it suits photographs and scans. PNG is lossless, so text and line art stay perfectly crisp and it can have a transparent background, but a page can be several times larger. If a JPEG of a text page looks fuzzy around the letters, raise the quality or the resolution, or switch to PNG.
  - q: What resolution and JPEG quality should I use?
    a: For reading on a screen, 150 DPI with Balanced quality is a good default. Use 96 or 72 DPI when you need small files for the web, and 300 DPI when the image will be printed or zoomed into. Quality between 70 and 85 percent is usually indistinguishable from the original on screen. Below 50 percent you will see blockiness around text. Doubling the DPI roughly quadruples the pixels and the file size.

## How pages become pictures

A PDF describes each page as drawing instructions: place this text in this font here, draw
this line, paint this image there. To make a picture, the browser's PDF engine carries out
those instructions onto a blank canvas the size of the page at your chosen resolution, then
the canvas is saved as a JPEG or PNG. Nothing about the PDF is guessed or approximated, so the
picture matches what a PDF viewer shows, including annotations such as highlights.

## Resolution decides the size

A PDF page is laid out at 72 points per inch, and DPI is how many pixels the picture uses for
each inch. An A4 page is about 595 × 842 points, so it becomes roughly 1240 × 1754 pixels at
150 DPI and 2480 × 3508 pixels at 300 DPI. More pixels mean a sharper picture and a bigger
file, so pick the lowest resolution that looks right where the image will be used.

## What powers this tool

Pages are drawn by [`pdf.js`](https://github.com/mozilla/pdf.js), the PDF engine built into
Firefox, which is loaded only once you choose a file. JPEG and PNG encoding use your
browser's own image encoder, and the ZIP is built with [`fflate`](https://github.com/101arrowz/fflate)
only when you press Download all.
