## Why placeholder text should be meaningless

Real, readable sentences pull a reviewer toward proofreading and rewording instead of
judging layout, spacing, and typography — which is usually the actual job at the mockup
stage. Lorem Ipsum's Latin-shaped nonsense gives text the right shape, length, and rhythm of
real copy (a mix of short and long words, natural sentence and paragraph lengths) without
being readable enough to distract from that.

## Checking how much placeholder text you actually generated

Design specs are often given as a word or character budget, not a paragraph count — the
word and character count shown next to the generated text covers that directly, so hitting
a specific budget doesn't take switching units back and forth by trial and error.
